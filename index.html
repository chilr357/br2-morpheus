BR2 Morpheus

BR2 Morpheus is the automation behind Chilr Productions' 24/7 YouTube streams of an AI fighter playing Bloody Roar 2 (PlayStation) and the Game Lab AI-vs-AI arena.

The software manages the channel's own live broadcasts, stream rollovers and live chat posts through the YouTube Data API. It is a private tool operated by the channel owner; it is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
